Central thesis (2026-06-10)

Simulator fidelity affects GAIN SELECTION more than GO/NOGO decisions. Simple-model gains cause 56% false rejection for gain-sensitive (FRAGILE) designs, and 12% false rejection for robust (EASY) designs — but 0% false approval. The gain sensitivity is a mechanical property: θ̈_max = T·sin(δ)·L/Iyy predicts FRAGILE with AUC=0.855, independent of wind, slew, or aerodynamics (H1–H4 all rejected, r≤0.034). A single test flight at Kp=2 confirms FRAGILE with AUC=0.947 and zero false alarms.
